Background and Theoretical Framework
The BSD conjecture, formulated by Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer in 1965 Notes on elliptic curves (II), posits a deep connection between the arithmetic of elliptic curves and the analytic behavior of their L-functions. This research hypothesizes an analogy in cosmology, where galaxy stellar masses, observed in large surveys like SDSS, exhibit a log-normal distribution. For instance, Kauffmann et al.
