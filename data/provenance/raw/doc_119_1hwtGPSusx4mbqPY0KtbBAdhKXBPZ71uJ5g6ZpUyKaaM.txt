MAGPHYS (version 06)

* Gas Content: The primary fuel for forming stars (though not directly provided in these datasets, proxies like dust mass can help).

* Galaxy Age: Younger galaxies typically exhibit higher SFR.
* Metallicity: Influences gas cooling and star formation efficiency.
