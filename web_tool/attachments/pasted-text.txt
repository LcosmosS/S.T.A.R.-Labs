Relativistic Thermodynamic Cohomology with Unified Point-Particle and Perfect-Fluid Matter Dynamics
A Variational Framework with Global Cohomological Charge, Local (A(\phi,\mathcal M,\mathcal J_Q)) Coupling, and a Conjectural Arithmetic–Cosmic Specialization

Abstract
We construct a variational framework, termed Relativistic Thermodynamic Cohomology (RTCH), in which thermodynamic state variables are represented by a map from spacetime into a differentiable target manifold and a closed, potentially non-exact two-form on that target induces a globally defined cohomological charge together with a local scalar invariant. The local invariant enters the matter sector through a universal conformal matter metric
A^2(\phi,\mathcal M,\mathcal J_Q)g_{\mu\nu},
]
where (\phi) is a scalar field, (\mathcal M) is a state-space scalar, and
\frac14F_{Q\mu\nu}F_Q^{\mu\nu},
\qquad
F_Q=\Phi^*\omega.
]
A central result is a unified matter theorem: the relativistic point-particle equation and the perfect-fluid Euler equation arise from the same universal matter coupling to (\tilde g_{\mu\nu}). The point-particle action is
[
S_p=-mc\int A,ds,
]
while the perfect-fluid realization is obtained from a Schutz–Sorkin-type action written entirely in terms of (\tilde g_{\mu\nu}). In the pressureless limit, the fluid acceleration equation reduces exactly to the point-particle equation.
The framework therefore establishes a precise relationship between microscopic and continuum matter descriptions without claiming that such a conformal unification is itself novel; conformal scalar-tensor theories already possess this general structural property. The potentially distinctive element is instead the proposed dependence of the conformal factor on the local scalar (\mathcal J_Q), where (\mathcal J_Q) originates from the pullback of a closed two-form carrying a global cohomological class.
We then formulate an explicitly conjectural specialization in which the state manifold and its differential forms are constructed from arithmetic invariants of elliptic curves. The Arithmetic–Cosmic Structure Conjecture (ACSC) and Global-to-Local Mapping Paradox Correction Theory (GLMPCT) are treated here as hypotheses requiring independent mathematical and empirical validation rather than as consequences of RTCH. In particular, no first-principles derivation is assumed to establish that elliptic-curve arithmetic must constitute the physical target manifold.
The paper therefore separates established differential-geometric identities, consequences of the proposed action, model assumptions, arithmetic conjectures, and empirical hypotheses. It develops null models, falsifiability criteria, observational constraints, and a research program designed to determine whether the (\mathcal J_Q)-dependent theory possesses physical content distinguishable from ordinary multi-field conformal gravity.

Computational limitations

Physical interpretation

Appendix G. Final Research Principle
The framework should be evaluated according to the following hierarchy:
[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
&\text{Is the mathematics nontrivial?}\
&\downarrow\
&\text{Does the action produce the claimed equations?}\
&\downarrow\
&\text{Is the new sector physically distinguishable?}\
&\downarrow\
&\text{Does it satisfy existing constraints?}\
&\downarrow\
&\text{Does it make successful out-of-sample predictions?}
\end{aligned}
}
]
Only after all five questions have been answered positively should the arithmetic–cosmic interpretation be promoted from conjectural specialization to a serious candidate physical hypothesis.
