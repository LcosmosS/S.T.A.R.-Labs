Detailed Examination of Verifying K = 1 for Log-Normal Mass Distributions
This section provides a comprehensive analysis of checking the finding that K = 1 for log-normal mass distributions with median M_0, using the MyTable_Bigsby.csv dataset, and its implications for testing Stellar_Mass2_Table.csv separately. The theory connects galaxy distributions to the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer (BSD) conjecture, positing a cosmological L-function L_{\text{cosmo}}(s) = \sum a_i^{-s}, where a_i = M_i / M_0, and a normalization constant K, hypothesized to approach 1 for large samples, drawing an analogy to BSD’s structure. We aim to validate this empirically, refine mathematically, justify theoretically, and assess statistically, evaluating proximity to grounded mathematics.
