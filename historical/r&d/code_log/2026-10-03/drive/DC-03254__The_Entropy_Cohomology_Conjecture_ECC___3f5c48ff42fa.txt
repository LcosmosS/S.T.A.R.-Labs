 L_grad = ‖∇M_pred − ∇M_true‖²

2. **Curvature Loss**:
 L_curv = ‖ω_pred − ω_true‖²

3. **Entropy Action**:
 L_action = ∫ (‖∇M‖² + λ‖Ric_M‖² + μ‖ω‖²) dV

4. **Topological Loss**:
 L_top = W_∞(Dgm_k(M_pred), Dgm_k(M_true))

**Composite Symbolic Loss**:
 L_total = α L_grad + β L_curv + γ L_action + δ L_top
