It is required that T appears in the scaling laws as a highly non-linear factor, possibly exponentiated or factorial, to ensure that the causality between the discrete arithmetic state and the continuous macroscopic parameters (V_{\text{Comove}}, \rho_{\text{Scale}}) is properly maintained.
