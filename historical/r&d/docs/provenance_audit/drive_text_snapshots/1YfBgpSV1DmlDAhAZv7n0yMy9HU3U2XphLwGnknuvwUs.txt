The Logic of Theory Evolution via Meta-Cohomological Topology of Explanatory Frameworks

This section introduces a formal structure for the logic of theory evolution within the ECC framework, using the machinery of meta-cohomological topology. The goal is to model how explanatory frameworks themselves evolve—how one symbolic theory deforms, mutates, or bifurcates into another—by defining topological continuity, stratified cohomology, and morphic descent at the meta-theoretic level.

1. Meta-Cohomological Space of Theories

Let 𝒯 denote the category of explanatory frameworks, where each object T ∈ Obj(𝒯) is a symbolic theory (e.g., ECC instance, symbolic recursion model, entropy balance formulation). Define the meta-cohomological topology on 𝒯 as:

- Open sets are collections of theories sharing stable cohomological invariants,
- Basis elements are defined by common harmonic sheaf kernels, persistent barcode features, or symbolic curvature profiles,
- Continuous maps preserve projection dynamics and cohomological rank across theory spaces.

2. Theoretical Coverings and Descent

Given a complex theory T, its decomposition into subtheories {T_i} defines a covering:

T = ⋃ T_i,

with transition morphisms f_{ij}: T_i → T_j satisfying cocycle conditions. A new theory T′ evolves by descent if:

- Cohomology H^*(T′) arises as the colimit of H^*(T_i),
- The explanatory power of T′ subsumes that of each T_i.

Descent encodes integrative synthesis, while cocycles represent compatibility conditions across explanatory strata.

3. Meta-Cohomology Groups

Define H^k_meta(𝒯) as the k-th meta-cohomology group:
- Measures the obstruction to lifting local theoretical data to global explanation,
- Encodes deformation classes of theories into higher-order explanatory spaces,
- Classifies recursive families of explanation by their conceptual curvature.

4. Logical Morphisms and Evolutionary Sequences

Let T₀ → T₁ → ... → Tₙ be a chain of explanatory frameworks connected by morphisms φ_i:
- Each φ_i preserves symbolic entropy structure and barcode mass,
- Chain maps induce long exact sequences in meta-cohomology,
- Non-trivial connecting homomorphisms correspond to explanatory mutation events.

Theory evolution follows exact sequences of form:

0 → H^0_meta(T₀) → H^0_meta(T₁) → H^1_meta(ker φ₁) → ...

5. Topological Invariants of Explanatory Evolution

Each theory T carries:
- A curvature field κ_T defining the explanatory tension,
- A sheaf ℱ_T of symbolic observables,
- Persistent cohomology class [ω_T] representing enduring explanatory structure.

Evolution preserves the class [ω_T] up to homotopy unless critical degeneracy or bifurcation occurs.

6. Moduli Space of Theories and Deformation Types

Let ℳ_𝒯 be the moduli space of all theories T under curvature-equivalence. The deformation type of a theory is its equivalence class under smooth symbolic transport:
- Connected components in ℳ_𝒯 define logical families,
- Boundary regions correspond to singularities, paradoxes, or degeneration.

The movement from one explanatory domain to another follows curvature-reducing meta-gradients.

7. Meta-Gradient Flow and Conceptual Thermodynamics

Define the entropy energy of a theory T:

E_meta(T) = ∫ ||∇_𝒯 ℳ_T||²,

where ℳ_T is the symbolic projection field in theory space. Evolution tends toward minima of E_meta, encoding theory convergence toward explanatory simplicity or symbolic saturation.

8. Conclusion

The logic of theory evolution in ECC is governed by a meta-cohomological topology: theories are not isolated points but cohomological objects embedded in a symbolic manifold of explanation. Their relationships—deformations, derivations, collapses—are captured by topological flows, categorical descent, and curvature gradients. Theories evolve not by intuition alone, but by curvature geometry and entropy balance in the meta-space of symbolic reasoning.
