I. Project Overview: Linking the BSD Conjecture and Cosmology


 * Core Research Question: The central question guiding this research is whether a meaningful connection can be established between the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer (BSD) conjecture (a concept from number theory) and cosmology, specifically through the distribution of galaxy stellar masses
 
 * Hypothesis: The primary hypothesis is that for large samples of galaxies, the normalization constant (K), derived from a cosmological L-function constructed using SDSS DR17 stellar mass data, approaches 


BSD_Conjecture_Cosmological_Map_Theory_Analysis (1).pdf]. This convergence suggests an analogy to the normalization behavior observed in the BSD conjecture 


[cite:BSD_Conjecture_Cosmological_Map_Theory_Analysis.pdf, BSD_Conjecture_Cosmological_Map_Theory_Analysis (1).pdf].


 * Reasoning:


   * The BSD conjecture posits a link between an elliptic curve's L-function's behavior at s=1 and the curve's algebraic rank [cite: BSD_Conjecture_Cosmological_Map_Theory_Analysis.pdf, BSD_Conjecture_Cosmological_Map_Theory_Analysis (1).pdf].
 
  * The normalization of the L-function reflects underlying arithmetic structures [cite: BSD_Conjecture_Cosmological_Map_Theory_Analysis.pdf, BSD_Conjecture_Cosmological_Map_Theory_Analysis (1).pdf].
  
 * By constructing a cosmological L-function and observing the behavior of its normalization constant, the research aims to draw parallels between these mathematical and cosmological concepts.
 
* 3D Universe Map:


   * The research also explores the construction of a 3D topographical map of the universe [cite: BSD_Conjecture_Cosmological_Map_Theory_Analysis.pdf, BSD_Conjecture_Cosmological_Map_Theory_Analysis (1).pdf].
 
  * This map aims to represent distances in light-years and density as topography [cite: BSD_Conjecture_Cosmological_Map_Theory_Analysis.pdf, BSD_Conjecture_Cosmological_Map_Theory_Analysis (1).pdf].
 
  * It seeks to preserve the natural size, shape, curvature, and distances of mapped objects [cite: BSD_Conjecture_Cosmological_Map_Theory_Analysis.pdf, BSD_Conjecture_Cosmological_Map_Theory_Analysis (1).pdf].


   * The Virgo cluster is used as a reference for scale [cite: BSD_Conjecture_Cosmological_Map_Theory_Analysis.pdf, BSD_Conjecture_Cosmological_Map_Theory_Analysis (1).pdf].
