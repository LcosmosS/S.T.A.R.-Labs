# Paper I — exact obstruction in Szekeres–Szafron dust

**Draft study question:** Does the complete preregistered elliptic *evolution* record uniquely determine full dust inhomogeneity in the registered nondegenerate class-I Szekeres–Szafron sector?

**Potential narrow claim (pending independent proof):** A regular pair of dust+Lambda metrics can share every frozen element of the Weierstrass evolution record yet differ in the invariant transverse density-gradient scalar J. Therefore that specific evolution record is insufficient for the *full matter field* in the registered class. Not a general obstruction to elliptic integrability or ACSC.

## Peer-review-ready manuscript sections and tests

1. **Prior literature and novelty:** compare derivation with Kraniotis–Whitehouse (arXiv:gr-qc/0105022), Szekeres solutions and invariant characterization by Coley–Layden–McNutt (DOI 10.1007/s10714-019-2647-6). Clearly isolate what is new vs classical Szekeres free data.
2. **Definition theorem:** freeze metric signature, Einstein sign/Lambda convention, full `E` including `M,K,f,epsilon,v0,sigma,g2,g3,Delta_wp`, entire domain, coordinate gauge and admissible solution class.
3. **General E1a derivation:** derive field equations and exact density, Weierstrass reduction and physical time/inverse-lattice branch without importing any desired special case. Failure is a frozen protocol-specification result, not something to silently fix.
4. **E1b pair and field equations:** independently derive both full Einstein tensors (including off-diagonals), regularity and two separate dust-density expressions with exact sign and energy conditions. Prove full elliptic-record agreement, not just equality of cubic invariants.
5. **Intrinsic nonisometry:** construct dust velocity spectrally from curvature at positive density, shear from covariant derivatives and repeated-eigenvalue 2-plane projector. Prove `J(S1)=0`, `J(S2)>0` on an open set, independent of comoving-coordinate residual gauge.
6. **Independent certificates:** exact symbolic checker, different numerical-autodiff checker, software versions and source-hashes, external human referee review of all assumptions. Spot checks corroborate, do not establish theorem.
7. **Scope, limitations and failure condition:** what Szekeres scalar does not capture; why it does not rule out observable-specific sufficiency (`M1-INH-E1c`) or different parent theories. Record counterexamples or sign errors permanently.

**Current evidence:** candidate exact symbolics + 4 independently implemented numerical field-equation spot checks. **Not yet externally peer-reviewed**, general E1a not accepted; theorem-level manuscript may not claim final proof.

**Suggested title (provisional):** *An elliptic-evolution insufficiency obstruction for inhomogeneous Szekeres–Szafron dust: an invariant constructive pair.*
